1.4 Background
In Barangay 133, Tondo, Manila, the team observed that communication between the
barangay officials and the residents is a significant challenge. The current system for sharing
information relies on traditional printed notices and posts on social media. However, these
methods are often ineffective. Printed notices can be easily missed or ignored, while social
media posts may not reach all residents due to algorithms or because not everyone follows
the page. This leads to missed announcements, confusion, and delayed updates.
A more specific problem identified in the community is the inefficient waste
management, particularly the "floating" garbage collection schedule. Residents are often
unsure of the exact time the garbage truck will pass by. This uncertainty causes two main
issues: first, residents leave their garbage out on the street for long hours, which attracts
pests, creates bad smells, and poses sanitation and health risks. Second, many residents miss
the collection entirely, leading to uncollected garbage piling up in the area.
The barangay currently lacks a centralized and real-time platform to handle these
issues. There is no official system where residents can receive instant, verified alerts and
where officials can efficiently manage services.
To address these problems, this study proposes the development of the “Multi-
Platform Barangay Announcement and Garbage Collection Notifier using IOT Technology.”
This project aims to provide a reliable, technology-based solution to improve communication
and streamline the garbage collection process, thereby helping Barangay 133 promote
cleanliness, order, and better community participation.

2.5 Business Rules
This section defines the business rules that govern the operation and management of
the Barangay Announcement and Garbage Collection Notifier using Image Processing. These
rules ensure the system operates consistently with barangay policies, maintains data privacy,
and supports reliable automated notifications
1. Account Creation and Management – Only the Super Admin is authorized to create,
update, and delete user and resident accounts. Residents are not permitted to self-register;
credentials are issued by Super Admin to ensure identity verification and system security.
2. Role-Based Access Control – System access and privileges are determined by role: Super
Admin (full system control), Barangay Official (manage announcements and feedback), and
Resident (view announcements, receive notifications, submit feedback). Role-based access
control enforces separation of duties and data protection.
3. Announcement Posting – Only authorized barangay officials or the Super Admin may
create, edit, or delete announcements. Every announcement must include a title, message of
body, and event date prior to publication.
4. Announcement Visibility – Announcements are displayed in chronological order with the
most recent first. Each announcement shall remain visible for a minimum of seven (7) days
unless removed by an authorized official.
5. Feedback Submission – Residents must be logged in to submit feedback. The system
restricts duplicate submissions for the same issue to prevent spam. Barangay officials are
expected to review and respond to feedback on a weekly basis.
Page | 36

---PAGE BREAK---

6. Image Processing Detection – The system’s image-processing module automatically
detects the presence of a garbage truck using CCTV footage.
7. Automatic Notification Trigger – When the image-processing module confirms garbage
truck detection, the system shall automatically send real-time notifications to all registered
residents. No manual activation is required, ensuring a fully automated process for efficiency
and reliability.
8. Data Privacy and Security – All personal and system data must comply with the Data Privacy
Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173). Sensitive information, including login credentials, shall
be encrypted and accessible only by authorized personnel.
9. System Availability – The system shall operate continuously, 24 hours a day, seven days a
week, except during scheduled maintenance. Barangay officials must announce any
maintenance periods in advance.
10. Detection Event Logging – Each garbage truck detection event shall be automatically
recorded with its timestamp, image reference, and notification status. This ensures
traceability of every detection process.
11. Ethical and Authorized Use – All users must adhere to ethical use of the system.
Tampering with data, posting false information, or misusing system features is strictly
prohibited and subject to disciplinary action.
2.6. Constraints
1. Image processing accuracy may vary depending on lighting conditions, camera
resolution, and angle of placement.
